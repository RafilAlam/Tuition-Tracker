# Tuition-Tracker  

**Track and Manage Tuition Payments**  
> A streamlined app to log, monitor, and manage tuition fees.
> I used this to keep track of my own tuition fees and managed to catch miscalculations and possible overcharges from my tuition center.

## Features  
- Logging of tuition payments  
- Due date tracking and payment history  
- Monthly summaries with formatted receipts
- Receipts are stored locally
